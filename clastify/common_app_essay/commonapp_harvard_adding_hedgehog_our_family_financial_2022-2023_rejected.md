> **A note on the food: The good news is that hedgehogs eat cat food, so we won't have to
>
> purchase specialty food for it. That said, we will need to shift our cat food purchasing strategy to

>
> currently purchase 16 lb./256 oz. bags at $11.88 for our two cats. Cats consume 4 oz. each per
