*Envelope:* In geometry, a curve that at every point touches one of the curves in the family. *Parabola:* Set of points equidistant from focus and directrix. General formula y=ax²+bx+c: focus at (−b/2a, (4ac−b²+1)/4a), directrix y=(4ac−b²−1)/4a.

*Ellipse:* Point set where sum of distances from two foci is constant (PF₁+PF₂=2a). General form x²/a²+y²/b²=1 (a>b>0): area S=πab, foci at (±√(a²−b²), 0).
