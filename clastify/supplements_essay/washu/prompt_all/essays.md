> My earliest memories of Haiti are steeped in sound and color. The clatter of jacks bouncing on the tiled floor as my sisters and I took turns, laughter echoing through the house. Twenty of us squeezing around the dinner table plated with griot and fried plantains, stuffing our faces before we go off to bed. > 
> Whether fostering familial bonds across borders or creating new spaces for connection, I’ve learned that true community transcends location. It’s a rhythm I carry—like the vibrant, unyielding beat of kompa, reminding me that home will always be with me no matter where I go.
