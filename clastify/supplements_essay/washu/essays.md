**申请专业：** 未指定  
**申请年份：** 2023/2024  
**学生数据：** SAT 1500

> When I first visited Morocco at age 12, I was completely out of my depth. The day I arrived, I clung to my mother like a newborn child and hid myself inside our guest room as much as possible. The first meal I had was couscous, a dish my mother made many times before. As I sat down, I realized something was off: the plates were small, and there weren't any utensils. I sat there confused, too afraid to speak, wedged between relatives I barely knew. There was no escape. I quickly recognized the 'couscous eating' conventions, observing from those around me, and soon became relaxed, listening and laughing with everyone else at the table. My visit to Morocco pushed me to seek discomfort more, leading to moments and memories I'll remember very fondly. It made me appreciate the unique backgrounds that every person has, and the power of food to bring them together: a truly communal act.
