> It was a three second change. Dozens of people cross the street everyday and most won't notice, but the magnitude of recognition does not correlate with the value of an action. My life is made easier hundreds of times a day by the work of unknown people. I repay them through actions in return. Currently, it's my war against car dependent cities.
