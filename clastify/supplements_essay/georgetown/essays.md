### 01 ✅ 真实录取
**申请专业：** Political Science And Government  
**申请年份：** 2024/2025  
**学生数据：** SAT 1390

> The activity I am most engaged in, both during school and summer, is serving as a volunteer firefighter in my community. Volunteering with Santa Fe County Fire Department provides me with a unique opportunity to handle high-pressure emergency situations, enhance my connection to my neighbors, and to be of assistance to the people around me in a profound way. Upon reflection, I begin to see how different it is from my other extracurricular pursuits and activities. In a counterintuitive manner, going on 911 calls can be somewhat calming for me. When my pager goes off, I feel a sense of composure and laser-focus set in. My list of priorities - often a jumbled mess of hours of homework and writing emails and working out and cleaning my room and more - takes second place to the simple task of donning my uniform and driving to the station. Emergency scenes can undoubtedly be hectic, disorganized, and often quite scary - however, the ability to focus all of my attention on a single, critical task is one that I cherish. Georgetown's service-oriented ethos resonates deeply with me, and the opportunity to volunteer with the on-campus EMS service only deepens my resolve to attend.

## As Georgetown is a diverse community, the Admissio (800 words)

> "As Georgetown is a diverse community, the Admissions Committee would like to know more about you in your own words. Please submit a brief personal or creative essay which you feel best describes you a"

## 样本

### 01 ✅ 真实录取
**申请专业：** Political Science And Government  
**申请年份：** 2024/2025  
**学生数据：** SAT 1390

> I can't name a single experience, or single "thing" that makes me, me. However, I believe every person is a sum of their lived experiences, and I do remember the most difficult challenge I have faced in my life. As my friends from the small Montessori elementary school I attended moved on to a private middle/high school that my family could not afford, I had to start fresh in Santa Fe Public Schools. The shock I faced was challenging to navigate. I had thus far lived a sheltered life, and suddenly I was meeting classmates with vastly different life experiences. One of my first friends at my new school shared her life stories, her traumas, and her account of her upbringing with me, and I was all of a sudden aware of the real-life presence of injustices that I had only read about in the newspaper. Initially, I was bitter about attending Mandela, the same school I've attended for more than a quarter of my life. However, it did not take long for me to see the value in my public education, uniting students of all backgrounds. As I aged through my 7th grade year, which was also the first year of the COVID pandemic, I came to love the diversity that surrounded me, realizing just how sheltered I had been and how small my worldview was. I owe my essence to New Mexico, and following Rep. Madrid's words, I look forward to being a changemaker in the state to which I owe my existence.

### 02 ✅ 真实录取
**申请专业：** 未指定  
**申请年份：** 2023/2024  
**学生数据：** SAT 1540

> As a five-year-old, I saw the movie "Cinderella," starring Brandy Norwood and Whitney Houston, two singers I admire. Both Houston, working behind the scenes as a producer, and Norwood as the titular character amazed me. Norwood held her own amongst established white actors, and I saw, for the first time, the result of allowing black people to express their talents without hindrance. Inspired by Brandy Norwood, I vowed to take charge of my life and embody a passion for excellence. Re-watching "Cinderella" when I was older made me realize that I've always wanted to be a star in my own life, but I've never wanted to be the only star. I appreciated Brandy Norwood for standing out in an industry that often disadvantages black creatives, but I did not want to be the princess that she was. I wanted to be Whitney Houston, who recognized the star within Brandy Norwood and facilitated her career growth.
