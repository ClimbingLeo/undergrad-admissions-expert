Being the God-fearing child I was, I took absolutely no chances and avidly avoided “The Finger” at all costs.
> 
> I spent years terrified of vengeance, especially in middle and high school when the motion became commonplace, and to be honest, I cannot pinpoint when exactly I realized.
> 
> What I had believed to be true for over a decade was a facade to prevent little kids from flicking others off.
> 
> Now, if I see a middle finger, I can’t help but laugh.
