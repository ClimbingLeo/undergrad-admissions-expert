> "Think about an academic subject that inspires you. Describe how you have furthered this interest inside and/or outside of the classroom."

## 样本

### 01 ✅ 真实录取
**申请专业：** Computer Science  
**申请年份：** 2024/2025  
**学生数据：** SAT 1560

> While the thrill of hitting a solution on a tricky algorithmic puzzle is always satisfying, what drew me into competitive programming was my admiration for algorithms and data structures. Every time I was introduced to a new problem-solving method, algorithm or data structure, I would marvel at the creativity of the mathematical proofs involved, ensuring my understanding through doodles on my notebook. Eventually, I began working through textbooks of algorithms, and specifically, graph theory, leaving me absolutely blown away by the innovative simplicity of the Max Flow-Min Cut theorem, and the surprisingly useful results derived from applying Linear Algebra to graphs. I found it elegant how these abstract and discrete structures could be used for logical proofs.
> 
> Leaning into this interest, I explored graph algorithms in even greater depth as I studied research papers, noting the various creative approaches to problem-solving and the clever proofs used to obtain results. I even experimented with blending techniques from these papers and competitive programming to optimize the presented algorithms. Although I never achieved any noteworthy results, the intellectual stimulation was sufficient to motivate me to seek out similar experiences.
> 
> In search of this, I attended the IIT Madras Open House event, where I was immediately captivated by the passionate research community and the innovative work in graph theory. Walking into Professor Nasre's office, I was, at first, taken aback by the spider web of graphs and equations scribbled all over the whiteboards. But after an engaging conversation with the professor about interesting graph problems and a career in graph theory research, I felt more inspired than ever to learn more about these problems. I contacted the professor soon after and began working as a research assistant, where I was assigned the task of performing a literature review on the Popular Matching problem and presenting my insights. 
> 
> Through this process, I became increasingly fascinated by the elegance of algorithms and computer science, gaining exposure to concepts like approximation algorithms and automata theory. I love how graph theory combines abstract thinking with practical problem-solving and am eager to continue exploring this field in college.

### 02 ✅ 真实录取
**申请专业：** Psychology  
**申请年份：** 2024/2025  
**学生数据：** SAT 1510

> "Why" is not just a series of comic books that I enjoyed reading as a child nor a rhetorical question that I pondered upon to the extent that it's quite my punchline. This habit further extended to questioning the reason for all my behaviors. Often fascinated by human cognition, my love for psychology began in my junior year when studying the IB psychology HL course.
> 
> From experimenting during the internal assessment to test the effect odor has on our memory to studying the effect of verbal cues on our memory, I found joy in testing different psychological hypotheses. Keeping in mind the ethical considerations led me to realize the tender disposition of humans. The uniqueness of every individual opened up a window for me to be more accepting and open-minded. 
> 
> Though not published, I even wrote a research paper on the topic of social stigma and its influence on the perception of mental health across Eastern and Western societies. Having experienced both societies, this topic was something of a fascination for me to investigate, while hoping to understand the diverse perspectives of the world. 
> 
> Furthermore, I often noticed the tendency of students to falsely label themselves with a mental disorder— commonly autism and OCD— after a social media post about the symptoms they stumbled upon. My friends also used to blatantly state that they felt depressed. As a psychology enthusiast, I couldn't just watch this as they did not seem to realize the severity of the worldwide issues. Utilizing the knowledge from the course, I spread awareness by putting up posters around the school, correcting their existing knowledge, giving a presentation in front of the school, and advocating for healthy mental health on the day of mental health day. The changes that I was able to observe in my community piqued my interest in the subject. 
> 
> Psychology has allowed me to view the world from a different lens, fostering my personality to be more open-minded, empathetic, and considerate. One day, I dream of continuing to test my imagination in an institution as a psychologist.

### 03 ✅ 真实录取
**申请专业：** Sociology  
**申请年份：** 2024/2025  
**学生数据：** SAT 1450

> Growing up in the Philippines, I witnessed pervasive poverty and the problems it causes among other children. My IB extended essay focused on poor national governance, and how it hurts the common man as seen in rice production. The history of the Philippines' agricultural sector encapsulates the dilemma facing the country. In the 1960s the Philippines was a net exporter of rice, but due to lack of investment in infrastructure, rapid population growth, and inefficient legislative policies, the country has fallen backward so far that it has become the largest rice importer in the world. Philippines rice self-sufficiency is a distant dream. Cheaper imported rice has destroyed livelihoods and impoverished domestic farmers, causing the abandonment of farmland and mass migration to urban centers, such as Manila.
> 
> Simultaneously to this research, I volunteered at Binhi English Literacy Foundation, an NGO that offers daily remedial classes for underprivileged children struggling in school. During my involvement, I was provided access to 12 years of student attrition data. My analysis showed, of course, that students with the highest rates of absences typically scored the lowest. More interestingly, the ones that were most frequently absent were often the poorest children, those who could not afford transportation to school or were required by their parents to work at home or on the streets to earn money for food that day. To these children, school was often seen as a luxury that they could not afford.
> 
> The Philippines is a developing country, with unmet physical and social infrastructure needs. The lack of educational opportunities for the poorest citizens perpetuates this cycle. Taking a macro perspective on the interplay between the economy and politics led me to question whether sufficient infrastructure investments are being made. Are more government programs needed to encourage children to stay in school? Is there possible legislation that I can advocate to encourage greater private investment that will help create livelihood opportunities at the community level? These questions are of utmost importance, which I will answer over the coming months.

### 01 ✅ 真实录取
**申请专业：** Computer Science  
**申请年份：** 2024/2025  
**学生数据：** SAT 1560

> The morning of my first hackathon, I contemplated participating—how could a sixth grade amateur possibly compete with high-school coding wizards? Understandably, I was terrified when another team approached me. Despite my nervous stuttering, the team was not discouraging me like I had expected; they were genuinely curious about my project. Their warm smiles and words of encouragement provided comfort as we gradually transitioned into zealous conversations about programming, technology, and our career aspirations. The seemingly intense competition broke away, and I saw the hackathon for what it really was: an open display of passion and communication. I was determined to help other young hackers have a similar experience.
> 
> At Oakridge Codefest, my school's overnight hackathon, I found an opportunity to do just that. Joining the core organizing team, I quickly noticed that the selection of winners was solely reliant on the technical skill of the participants in its scoring system. While this was definitely relevant in a programming competition, this approach discouraged the collaborative and supportive atmosphere I had valued so highly. To combat this, I revamped the scoring system, introducing "Point-based scoring" in which teams earn points by participating in various collaborative mini challenges. 
> 
> I trained volunteers to actively encourage participants by creating guidelines to provide hints to participants who were stuck, and guiding younger students to help them finish their projects. As the hackathon ended, participants from across teams all gathered together in excited discussions, not about scores or rankings, but about their ideas, their interests, and their love for technology. After the event, the conversations carried on into our discord server, soon filled with questions from newbies, guidance from veterans and silly programmer humor.
> 
> I am proud of the experience I helped build: facilitating the exchange of ideas, the birth of new friendships, and the creation of the tech-loving Oakridge Codefest community.

> At the age of 11, I moved to India. Due to this sudden change in my lifestyle, I had extreme struggles to adapt to this foreign land. All sorts of challenges came my way—language barrier, climate, food, and so much more. 
>

Then it led to the penultimate moment where I had the privilege to campaign for Head of student council for my school. I won. I seized this opportunity to further help them-- from giving speeches about Korea on occasion to raising awareness to suppress racism, and even raising concerns that the Koreans had during student council meetings since language was the biggest barrier, I realized that my efforts didn't go in vain seeing the smiles on the faces of the Korean kids at the school, giving me a replete sensation never-felt anywhere else. 
> 
> The Korean community continues to flourish. Thereby, I hope that my small efforts will lead to a butterfly effect, encouraging and inspiring others to reach out to the ones who are struggling.

### 03 ✅ 真实录取
**申请专业：** Sociology  
**申请年份：** 2024/2025  
**学生数据：** SAT 1450

> To use a World Bank term, the Philippines has become "learning poor." Embarrassingly, according to the World Bank, approximately 90% of Filipino children aged 10 cannot read and comprehend simple texts - a staggering statistic that highlights the educational crisis in my country.
> 
> I found that offering food is a significant incentive for parents to send their children to school. Research I conducted yielded the conclusion that providing meals in schools can increase attendance rates by up to 20%. After quantifying the sheer impact of food on educational motives, I authored a paper presenting the vital importance of feeding programs on education motivation to the Binhi English Literacy Board. Through this, we advocated a joint feeding/literacy initiative with another NGO. This led to six separate feeding programs impacting nearly 200 students.
> 
> During the COVID-19 pandemic, I witnessed the egregious displays of educational shortcomings for many students across the country. Students were provided erroneous and outdated educational materials, while simultaneously being participants in one of the longest lockdowns globally. Consequently, this created an astronomical educational gap, which especially depressed student engagement and motivation.
> 
> As an eight-year volunteer for the orphanage Virlanie Foundation, I've had the pleasure of tutoring special needs children, most recently to recuperate lost opportunities during the pandemic. Every time without fail, engagement and attendance would always spike with the promise of snacks and food following the completion of activities.
> 
> Seeing how differing social values impact the trajectories of communities, has made me want to pursue cultural anthropology academically. Building bridges depends first on meeting people where they are. Improving the effectiveness of schools, health programs, reskilling, and other livelihood initiatives requires a nuanced understanding of what matters most to the beneficiaries and how to motivate them to engage productively. I sincerely hope to continue to champion educational initiatives that resonate with local needs, ensuring that every child has the opportunity to thrive and reach their fullest potential.
