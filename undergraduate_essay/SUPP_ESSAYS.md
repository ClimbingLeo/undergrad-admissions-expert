# 小文书案例库（Supplemental Essays）
**用途：** 供 agent 学习各校小文书的写作逻辑、题目策略与文体风格
**来源：** 学生本人公开发布（Medium/GitHub）+ 官方 Admissions Blog
**更新：** 2026年4月（v1.0）

---

## ⚠️ 与主文书案例库的区别

| 维度 | 主文书（Common App Personal Statement） | 小文书（Supplemental Essays） |
|---|---|---|
| 字数 | 650字 | 50–500字不等（多数250字以内） |
| 数量 | 每人1篇 | 每校1–8道题，申请10校=10–30篇 |
| 内容策略 | 展示核心人物形象（"我是谁"） | 展示匹配性（"为什么是你 + 为什么是这所学校"） |
| 评价维度 | 叙事、文笔、真实性 | 具体性、独特性、与学校文化的契合度 |
| 常见失误 | 宏大叙事、简历复读 | Why School太泛、短文太浅/太刻意展示 |

---

## ⚠️ 版权/复用提醒

本库中的完整文书仅供内部学习结构、选题和写法。对学生或对外输出时，优先使用链接、摘要、结构分析和少量短摘录，避免大段复述原文。

## 一、各校小文书 Prompt 汇总（2025/2026届，需按官网复核）

> 注：本节只作为写作规划索引。各校题目每年可能微调，给学生定稿前必须以当年申请系统或官网为准。

### Stanford（3道短文 + 若干50字短答）
| 题号 | Prompt | 字数 |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | The Stanford community is deeply curious and driven to learn in and out of the classroom. Reflect on an idea or experience that makes you genuinely excited about learning. | 250字 |
| S2 | Virtually all of Stanford's undergraduates live on campus. Write a note to your future roommate that reveals something about you or that will help your roommate — and us — know you better. | 250字 |
| S3 | Please describe what aspects of your life experiences, interests and character would help you make a distinctive contribution as an undergraduate to Stanford University. | 250字 |
| S4+ | Several short questions | 50字以内 |

### MIT（5道短文 + 附加信息）
| 题号 | Prompt | 字数 |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | What field of study appeals to you the most right now? Tell us more about why this field of study at MIT appeals to you. | 约100-200字 |
| M2 | We know you lead a busy life, full of activities, many of which are required of you. Tell us about something you do simply for the pleasure of it. | 约100-200字 |
| M3 | While some reach their goals following well-trodden paths, others blaze their own trails achieving the unexpected. In what ways have you done something different than what was expected in your educational journey? | 约100-200字 |
| M4 | MIT brings people with diverse backgrounds together to collaborate, from tackling the world's biggest challenges to lending a helping hand. Describe one way you have collaborated with others to learn from them, with them, or contribute to your community together. | 约100-200字 |
| M5 | How did you manage a situation or challenge that you didn't expect? What did you learn from it? | 约100-200字 |
| M6 | Additional information（可选） | 按申请系统 |

### UChicago（Uncommon Essay + Why UChicago）
- **Uncommon Essay**：每年换题，允许选择历史题目或自拟题目，核心考察创意、思维方式和文体控制。
- **Why UChicago**：通常围绕为什么该校的 Core、学术文化、城市资源和具体项目适合申请者；必须避免泛泛堆资源名。

### Yale（学术短答 + Why Yale + short takes + 400字短文）
- 学术兴趣：选择最多3个 academic areas，并解释一个相关主题/想法（200字以内）
- Why Yale：兴趣、价值观、经历如何把你带向 Yale（125字以内）
- Short takes：4道，每道约200字符，如 "What inspires you?" 等
- Essay：3选1，400字以内，主题包括 opposing view、community、personal experience

### Princeton（学术题 + Your Voice + More About You）
- 学术题：A.B./undecided 与 B.S.E. 题目不同，围绕学术兴趣和 Princeton 资源匹配。
- Your Voice：通常包含 lived experience/community 类长题和 service/civic engagement 类题目。
- More About You：短答题，展示人格、品味和非简历信息。
- 另需提交 graded written paper。

**小文书策略分析**

| 维度 | 分析 |
|---|---|
| **整体叙事一致性** | 所有小文书都在强化同一个人物形象："科技创业者+有社会责任感的服务者+Apple信徒"。从 Minecraft 到 Cristo Rey 到 Jobs keynote，一以贯之 |
| **Roommate 策略** | 用 Top 10 List 打破常规，展示了人格（幽默/自嘲）而非成就；"I will play Minecraft. / Fair warning: I will probably make you play Minecraft." 是最memorable的两条 |
| **Intellectual Vitality 策略** | 没有从课堂谈起，而是把创业经历包装成"跨学科学习实验室"，展示了 meta-learning 能力 |
| **What Matters 策略** | 用服务主题与 Intellectual Vitality 的创业主题形成互补，展示了"不只是会写代码的人" |
| **短题策略** | 50字短题全部与核心身份挂钩（Jobs/Apple/编程）或展示真实人格；没有尝试去讨好或过度展示 |
| **关键写法** | 具体数字（100,000用户，300个玩具，50-60小时/周，4个夏天）让所有描述可信可触摸 |

#### UChicago 小文书全文（作者本人 Medium 公开，完整全文）

**Prompt（2024届 UChicago Uncommon Essay）：**
*The apple that fell from a Malus pumila tree and bonked Newton on the head inspired him to study gravity. What is something that bonked you on the head and caused you to explore and reach new growths? (around 650 words)*

> He never looked like that before. Bryan, my English tutor, had been to our house many times…but not like that. Ogling at his hair, the sparkles of his shirt penetrated through my eyes and into my soul. It made for an interesting conversation piece, anyway. Before this spectacle, Bryan wore regular clothes, trousers, and a golf shirt, clean-cut. Now, standing before us, he had morphed into a bushy-bearded man in sparkly pink clothing and a rainbow for hair. When I was ten, a twisted idea of masculinity emerged. I say "twisted" with the best possible connotation.
>
> That same year, in a life-changing move, I come from a conservative Chinese upbringing, so the thought of a "Brony", an adult MLP fan, was a shock to my system. Men are to be strong, wear suits, thick-skinned, and stoic. Then there was Bryan, enthusiastically fanning through his photos from the convention, where everyone rocked rainbows and ponies and sparkles, with even more egregious attire and make-up. Speechless, I felt all my assumptions about masculinity shaken by a pink shirt and rainbow hair. As I grew older, and without his knowledge, I began to understand why Bryan felt comfortable expressing himself this way.
>
> What the heck was My Little Pony? I thought. All my questions were answered soon enough. The recounting of this tale is not necessarily an endorsement for My Little Pony. It is, however, the first time my gender norms were challenged.
>
> Subsequently, I started watching episodes regularly, at first catching up, eventually following the series until it ended in 2019. For a long time, I couldn't pinpoint exactly why I watched MLP. It wasn't until the realization that I watched the show whenever school and life was at its most stressful that I concluded it was for their softness, the way ponies freely expressed their feelings, the sincerity in their apologies, and the show's fuzzy main theme of friendship. I once exuded a tough exterior at school, misguidedly trying to be what society considered a man; not expressing myself simply because I'm prescribed a boy. I felt like I was trying to subdue part of what makes me, me.
>
> MLP, weirdly, taught me it was okay to embrace my feelings. When I'm stressed, I express it. When I'm with friends, I genuinely let myself go. Once I started opening up and dropped the aloof and cold front, I met people with whom I'm still friends today, made memories I still cherish and laugh about, and formed bonds that I hope to carry for life. More importantly, I became my authentic self.

**小文书策略分析**

| 维度 | 分析 |
|---|---|
| **题目类型** | UChicago Uncommon Essay：完全开放式创意写作，考察的是"有没有独特视角+能不能写"，不是"你有多厉害" |
| **开场白策略** | 用Bryan的外貌转变设置悬念，读者被迫追问"发生了什么"，前两句就锁定注意力 |
| **核心矛盾** | 保守华人家庭背景 vs. Brony文化冲击 → 这个张力本身就是最有力的叙事引擎 |
| **主题深度** | 表面写 My Little Pony，实际写的是：gender norms（性别规范）、authentic self（真实自我）、由外部冲击触发的内省成长 |
| **适合UChicago的原因** | UChicago 欣赏intellectual risk-taking（智识冒险）；这篇文章选题奇特但内核严肃，完全符合该校"不装、真实、有深度"的文化气质 |
| **注意事项** | 这篇文章有一定的性别表达主题（男生写接受柔软/感性）；在文化更保守的语境下（如部分中国国际生背景）需要评估是否适合移植 |

## 三、补充：小文书写法模式速查（来自 CollegeEssayGuy 分析）

以下为 CollegeEssayGuy 对 Stanford 历史版小文书各题的写法模式总结（非真实案例，为策略指导）。其中 Historical Moment、Society Challenge、Extracurricular Elaboration 等题型不应默认视为当前申请季题目；定稿前必须按官网/申请系统复核。

### Stanford 短题（50字以内）写法模式

**Historical Moment — 高分写法特征：**
- 选一个极具体的时刻，不要选宏观事件（"二战"太泛）
- 最好能体现你的个人身份（内行人视角）
- 幽默可以是加分项
- 示例："I want to watch George Washington go shopping. I have an obsession with presidential trivia, and the ivory-gummed general is far and away my favorite. Great leaders aren't necessarily defined by their moments under pressure; sometimes tiny decisions are most telling — like knickers or pantaloons?"（效果：幽默，但展示了"对历史细节的真实痴迷"）

**Society Challenge — 高分写法特征：**
- 提出一个你真正在意的问题，不要选"答案显而易见"的话题
- 可以用提问+立场的结构
- 示例："We are trying to police AI to mitigate misuse; however, how do we even begin defining misuse? These questions belong not in computer science but in sociology and ethics. As I learnt with Stanford SKY, we need interdisciplinary committees to ensure standards are made in human interests, not profit incentives."

**Extracurricular Elaboration（150字）— 高分写法特征：**
- 一定要写主文书和活动列表里还没覆盖到的内容
- 展示过程、投入度、具体感受，而非成就清单
- 示例："Falafels. Construction Work. Wave-Particle Duality. These describe my train ride for two hours every Saturday to attend the Columbia Science Honors Program."（不完整句开场，制造视觉节奏感）

### Stanford 250字题写法模式

**Intellectual Vitality — 高分写法特征：**
- 从一个非常具体的 idea 切入（不是"经历"）
- 前50字说清楚这个 idea 是什么
- 要把这个抽象 idea 连回到自己
- 示例核心："What's more probable: dying from a shark attack, or dying from falling airplane parts? Surprisingly, the answer is falling airplane parts. But why does our intuition point us to [shark attacks]?"（以反常识的概率悖论引入，展示 statistical thinking 的乐趣）

**Roommate Note — 高分写法特征：**
- 展示"人"而非成就
- 可以用list、信件、对话等非标准格式
- 加入具体的quirks和行为细节（"I will play Minecraft" 比 "I love technology" 更真实）
- 要有独特的声音/语气（humor, warmth, or wit）

## 四、小文书 vs 主文书选题避坑表

| 常见失误 | 具体表现 | 后果 |
|---|---|---|
| **Why School 太泛** | "I love Stanford's diverse campus and amazing professors" | 可以套用任何学校，毫无差异性 |
| **短题太刻意** | 50字里硬塞3个成就 | 读起来像简历，没有人格温度 |
| **把主文书内容搬进小文书** | Intellectual Vitality 写的和主文书完全相同的经历 | 浪费展示机会，显示视角单一 |
| **过度谦虚型短题** | "I'm just a normal student who..." | 50字的黄金机会就这么没了 |
| **UChicago Uncommon Essay 写成作文** | 回答结构工整，像语文考试议论文 | 完全不符合 UChicago 的阅卷期待 |
| **Roommate Note 写成自我介绍** | 罗列背景信息和成就 | 错过展示性格/quirks的唯一机会 |
