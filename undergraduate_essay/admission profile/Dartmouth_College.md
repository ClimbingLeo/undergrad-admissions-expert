- "Dartmouth has practiced holistic admissions since 1921"，并强调对「the whole person」的考量（[Dartmouth 标化 FAQ](https://home.dartmouth.edu/news/sat-reactivate-faqs)）。
- 同伴推荐信用来从另一个角度了解申请人的 "interests and character from another perspective"（[Peer Recommendation](https://admissions.dartmouth.edu/glossary-term/peer-recommendation)）。

归纳（基于以上官方信息）：
- 文书、推荐信、活动、品格全部是 Very important，权重不低于成绩。官方「强烈鼓励」同伴推荐信，说明它很在意「同龄人眼中的你」。

- 补充文书大量围绕对话、分歧、差异（Goodall 题、Kermit 题），与学校强调的 dialogue 文化一致。

## 文书题目（2026–27 申请季）

来源：[Dartmouth Writing Supplement（Class of 2031，2026-07-22 更新）](https://admissions.dartmouth.edu/glossary-term/writing-supplement)。另需 Common App 主文书。

**题 1（必答，100 词以内）**
> As you seek admission to Dartmouth's Class of 2031, what aspects of the college's academic program, community, and/or campus environment attract your interest? How is Dartmouth a good fit for you?

考什么：100 词的 Why Dartmouth，要具体到学术项目、社区或环境，并说清楚「为什么适合你」。

**题 2（必答，二选一，250 词以内）**
- A. There is a Quaker saying: Let your life speak. Describe the environment in which you were raised and the impact it has had on the person you are today.
- B. "Be yourself," Oscar Wilde advised. "Everyone else is taken." Introduce yourself.

考什么：成长环境如何塑造你（A），或直接的自我介绍（B），都是看你是谁。

**题 3（必答，六选一，250 词以内）**
- A. What excites you?
- B. Labor leader and civil rights activist Dolores Huerta recommended a life of purpose. "We must use our lives to make the world a better place to live, not just to acquire things," she said. "That is what we are put on the earth for." In what ways do you hope to make—or are you already making—an impact? Why? How?
- C. In an Instagram post, best-selling British author Matt Haig cheered the impact of reading. "A good novel is the best invention humans have ever created for imagining other lives," he wrote. How have you experienced such insight from reading? What did you read and how did it alter the way you understand yourself and others?
- D. The social and family interactions of wild chimpanzees have been the focus of Dame Jane Goodall's research for decades. Her understanding of animal behavior prompted the English primatologist to see a lesson for human communities as well: "Change happens by listening and then starting a dialogue with the people who are doing something you don't believe is right." Channel Dame Goodall: Tell us about a moment when you engaged in a difficult conversation or encountered someone with an opinion or perspective that was different from your own. How did you find common ground?
- E. Celebrate your nerdy side.
- F. "It's not easy being green…" was the frequent refrain of Kermit the Frog. How has difference been a part of your life, and how have you embraced it as part of your identity, outlook, or sense of purpose?

考什么：A/E 看真实的智识热情；B 看行动和影响；C 看阅读带来的共情与自我认知；D 看能否与不同观点的人对话、找到共同点；F 看你如何对待自己的「不同」。

**体现在申请设计中**：

- 同伴推荐信（peer recommendation）strongly encouraged，同档学校中少见。
- 必答六选一文书中有「一次艰难对话或遇到不同观点、如何找到共同点」和「差异如何成为你身份的一部分」两个选项。
- CDS 2025–26 C7：Character/personal qualities 为 Very important，Talent/ability 只是 Important；校友面试可选，官方称 "both informative and evaluative"。

**流行说法核查**：

- 「达特茅斯看重社区感、户外和本科教学」→ **部分支持**。社区感支持：招生院长多年以社区为选拔框架，申请设计有同伴推荐信；本科教学部分支持：使命写 "a faculty dedicated to teaching"，但招生办没有说偏好看重本科教学的申请者；户外只作为校园特质成立，没有官方表述把户外经历当加分项。

**对申请者的含义（归纳）**：

1. 品格要能被别人看见：同伴推荐信和文书里写你在团队、课堂里如何对待他人，比自述「我很善良」更有说服力。
2. 户外经历可以作为「为什么适合 Dartmouth」的素材，但不是门槛。

## 对中国学生的建议

1. **申请资助不影响录取**：Dartmouth 对国际生 need-blind 并满足全部需求，有资助需求的家庭不必为了录取而放弃申请资助。这一点与 Duke 等对国际生 need-aware 的学校相反，选校时可作为重要考量。
2. **标化必须交，但有替代路径**：Dartmouth 恢复了强制标化。国际课程学生可以用 3 门 AP 或 IB 预估分 / A-Level 成绩满足要求。有 SAT 的学生仍建议提交，因为标化是 Very important，且看的是「放在本校背景里的相对水平」。
3. **英语成绩是硬要求**：非英语授课不满两年必须交，多数成功申请者 TOEFL 100+ / IELTS 7+ / Duolingo 135+，按这个标准准备。
4. **ED 杠杆明显**：按 CDS，Class of 2029 ED 约 21.6% vs 整体约 6.0%，ED 录取约占总录取的近一半。确定首选 Dartmouth 的学生应优先考虑 ED。
5. **一定要交同伴推荐信**：官方 strongly encouraged，推荐信又是 Very important。建议找真正一起做过项目、组过队的同学，写出具体的合作细节。
6. **面试不保证有**：校友面试是 evaluative 的，但属可选、没面试不吃亏；官方鼓励提交 InitialView，可作为补充。
7. **文书要贴合「对话」文化**：题 3 的 Goodall 题和 F 题都和对话、差异有关，录取生里约 1/3 在 Why Dartmouth 中提到重视言论自由与对话的文化。Why Dartmouth 只有 100 词，要写具体的学术或社区资源，不要写空话。
